It's now in a phase for bug fixes and adding methods to use the battle engine in requested ways by other modders.

This mod, alongside national dex, modernizes gen1recomp's gen 1 and gen 2 games battle mechanics to up to generation 9 pokemon.

g9-battle-engine in specific is a battle simulator inspired and based on pokemon showdown's combat engine.

Including weathers, fields, terrains, held items, gimmicks (mega, tera, zmove, dyna/giga), entry hazards, rooms, dynamic speed based turns,
modernized stats, save of modern stats per pokemon.
